A little about us

Good growing.
Honest relationships.

Unifisher Farming is an agricultural venture based in Saipur, Kolkata, West Bengal. We focus on mushroom cultivation and providing customers with quality produce and dependable service.

We believe good food begins with care: attention to growing practices, product handling, and the people we serve. We continue to grow with a long-term view of quality and environmental responsibility.

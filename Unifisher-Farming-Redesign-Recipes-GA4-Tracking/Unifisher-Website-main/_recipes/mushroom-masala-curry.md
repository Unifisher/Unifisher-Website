ingredients:
  - "250 g mushrooms, cleaned and halved or sliced"
  - "2 tablespoons oil"
  - "1 medium onion, finely chopped"
  - "2 tomatoes, chopped or blended"
  - "1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste"
  - "½ teaspoon turmeric"
  - "1 teaspoon ground coriander"
  - "½ teaspoon cumin"
  - "½ teaspoon garam masala"
  - "½ teaspoon chilli powder, or to taste"
  - "Salt and water, as needed"
  - "Fresh coriander to garnish"
steps:
  - "Heat oil in a pan and cook the onion until soft and lightly golden."
  - "Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 30 seconds."
  - "Add tomatoes, turmeric, coriander, cumin, chilli powder and salt. Cook until the masala thickens and the oil begins to separate."
  - "Add mushrooms and stir to coat them in the masala. Cook for 5 minutes."
  - "Add a little water to make gravy, cover and simmer until mushrooms are tender and cooked through."
  - "Stir in garam masala, garnish with coriander and serve with rice or flatbread."

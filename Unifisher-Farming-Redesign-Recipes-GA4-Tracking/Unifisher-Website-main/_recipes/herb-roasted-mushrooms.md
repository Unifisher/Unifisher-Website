ingredients:
  - "300 g mushrooms, cleaned and halved if large"
  - "1½ tablespoons olive oil"
  - "2 garlic cloves, finely chopped"
  - "1 teaspoon dried thyme or mixed herbs"
  - "Salt and black pepper"
  - "1 teaspoon lemon juice"
  - "Fresh parsley, to finish"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220°C. Line a baking tray."
  - "Toss mushrooms with olive oil, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper."
  - "Spread in a single layer so the mushrooms roast rather than steam."
  - "Roast for 15–20 minutes, turning once, until tender and browned."
  - "Finish with lemon juice and chopped parsley. Serve as a side or add to toast, pasta or grain bowls."

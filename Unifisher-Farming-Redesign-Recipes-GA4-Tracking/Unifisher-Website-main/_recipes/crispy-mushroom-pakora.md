ingredients:
  - "200 g mushrooms, cleaned and sliced"
  - "¾ cup gram flour (besan)"
  - "2 tablespoons rice flour (optional, for extra crispness)"
  - "½ teaspoon turmeric"
  - "½ teaspoon chilli powder"
  - "½ teaspoon ajwain or cumin seeds"
  - "2 tablespoons chopped coriander"
  - "Salt to taste"
  - "Water, as needed"
  - "Oil for frying"
steps:
  - "Mix gram flour, rice flour, turmeric, chilli powder, ajwain, coriander and salt in a bowl."
  - "Add water gradually to make a thick coating batter."
  - "Add mushrooms and toss until coated."
  - "Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Fry small batches until golden and cooked through, turning as needed."
  - "Drain on a rack or paper towel and serve hot with green chutney. Take care around hot oil."

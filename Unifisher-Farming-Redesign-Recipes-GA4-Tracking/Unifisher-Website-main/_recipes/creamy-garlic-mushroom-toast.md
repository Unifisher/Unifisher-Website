ingredients:
  - "200 g mushrooms, sliced"
  - "1 tablespoon butter or olive oil"
  - "2 garlic cloves, finely chopped"
  - "¼ cup cream or unsweetened cooking cream"
  - "2 tablespoons grated cheese (optional)"
  - "Salt and black pepper"
  - "2–4 slices of bread"
  - "Parsley or coriander, chopped"
steps:
  - "Toast the bread until crisp and set aside."
  - "Heat butter or oil in a pan. Add mushrooms and cook until browned and tender."
  - "Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds."
  - "Lower the heat and stir in cream and cheese, if using. Simmer gently until the sauce coats the mushrooms."
  - "Season with salt and black pepper. Spoon over toast and garnish with herbs."

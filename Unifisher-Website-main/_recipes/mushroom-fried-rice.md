ingredients:
  - "2 cups cooked, chilled rice"
  - "200 g mushrooms, sliced"
  - "1 tablespoon neutral oil"
  - "2 garlic cloves, chopped"
  - "½ cup mixed vegetables"
  - "2 spring onions, sliced"
  - "1–2 tablespoons soy sauce"
  - "Black pepper to taste"
  - "Optional: scrambled egg or firm tofu"
steps:
  - "Break up the cold cooked rice so there are no large clumps."
  - "Heat oil in a wide pan or wok. Cook mushrooms until browned and moisture has evaporated."
  - "Add garlic and mixed vegetables; stir-fry until just tender."
  - "Add rice and toss over high heat until hot throughout."
  - "Add soy sauce and pepper. Fold in spring onions and optional cooked egg or tofu."
  - "Serve immediately. Refrigerate leftovers promptly and reheat thoroughly."

ingredients:
  - "200 g oyster mushrooms, cleaned and torn into bite-size pieces"
  - "1 tablespoon oil"
  - "4 garlic cloves, finely chopped"
  - "1 small onion, sliced"
  - "1 small capsicum, sliced (optional)"
  - "1–2 green chillies, chopped"
  - "1 tablespoon soy sauce"
  - "1 teaspoon vinegar or lemon juice"
  - "Black pepper and salt, to taste"
  - "Spring onion or coriander, to finish"
steps:
  - "Wipe the mushrooms clean and tear larger clusters into even pieces."
  - "Heat the oil in a wide pan. Add garlic and green chilli; stir for 20–30 seconds without browning."
  - "Add onion and capsicum, if using, and stir-fry for 2 minutes."
  - "Add mushrooms and cook over medium-high heat until tender and excess moisture has evaporated, about 5–7 minutes."
  - "Stir in soy sauce, vinegar or lemon juice, pepper and a little salt. Taste before adding more salt."
  - "Finish with spring onion or coriander and serve hot with rice or noodles."
